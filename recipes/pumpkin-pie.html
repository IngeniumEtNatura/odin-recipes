The Best Friendsgiving Pumpkin Pie

About This Recipe

This pumpkin pie is the absolute best recipe that I've ever made. The rustic, crumbly pastry is contrasted with a soft, velvety filling for indulgent flavors in every bite. This recipe is the perfect addition to your Friendsgiving roster and will have your friends eagerly awaiting it each year.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium-sized delica pumpkin
  • 3 large free-range eggs
  • 175g Domino golden sugar
  • 3 teaspoons vanilla bean paste
  • 100mL double cream
  • 3/4 of a teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 a teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 of a teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C/350°F. Place the pumpkin on a baking tray, carefully pierce once or twice with the tip of a sharp knife, then bake in the oven for 1 hour 30 minutes, or until golden and very soft. Transfer to a board to cool completely.
  2. Once the pumpkin is cool enough to handle, remove the stalk and, using a kinfe and tongs, carefully slice lengthways, down the middle. Scoop out the seeds and put aside for tasty cooking another day. Then weigh out 400-450g of pumpkin flesh.
  3. Whiz the flesh in a food processor until smooth with the egg yolks (reserving the whites for later), sugar, vanilla bean paste, double cream, ground spices, a few good gratings of nutmeg, and a pinch of sea salt.
  4. In a clean bowl, whisk the egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff, glossy peaks form. Fold the pumpkin mixture into the egg whites and pour into the pie crust.
  5. Bake at the bottom of the oven for 45 minutes, or until th filling is set and the top slightly cracked. Transfer to a wire rack and leave to cool completely.
