Gluten-Free Skillet Cornbread

About This Recipe

This is a take on a traditional Southern cornbread: forged in a skillet, blessed by bacon fat, and free of sugar. While flour isn't typical of cornbread from the Mountain South, some has been added to ensure it holds together. This recipe has also been modified to be gluten-free and uses King Arthur Gluten-Free Measure for Measure Flour. Also be sure to use fresh liquid buttermilk to preserve the flavor of the recipe!

Ingredients

  • 50g bacon fat or beef tallow
  • 245g whole cornmeal
  • 60g King Arthur Gluten-Free Measure for Measure flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon table salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 340g buttermilk, well shaken

Instructions

  1. Put the bacon fat in 9" or 10" cast iron skillet and place in the oven as it preheats to 450°F.
  2. Whisk together the cornmeal, four, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg and buttermilk. Make a well in the center of the cornmeal micture and pour in the egg mixture. Stir only until blended.
  4. Remove the skillet from the oven and pour in the batter. The skillet will be hot and the batter will sizzle and pop so be careful.
  5. Bake until the cornbread is firm in the middle and golden brown on top, about 25 minutes. Turn out onto a cutting board or cooling rack and serve hot.
